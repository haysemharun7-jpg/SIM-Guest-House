Our Story

A home
away from home

Nestled in the heart of Addis Ababa, SIM Guest House Hotel was founded with a singular vision — to offer discerning travelers a sanctuary of understated luxury, warm hospitality, and authentic Ethiopian character.

Every detail of SIM has been thoughtfully curated, from our handpicked furnishings to the locally sourced ingredients in our kitchen. We believe that great accommodation is more than a bed — it is an experience that stays with you long after you check out.
